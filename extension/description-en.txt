Copies text from LibreOffice Writer to the PJe editor, preserving formatting.

Supported features:

• Alignment and indentation proportional to the original measurements;
• Bold, italics and underline;
• Font colors and highlighting;
• Superscript and subscript;
• Numbered and bulleted lists, including multiple levels of nested lists;
• Tables with merged cells, dimensions, alignment, borders and fill colors;
• Embedded images, including sizing, positioning, margins, cropping and rotation;
• Hyperlinks that open in a new tab;
• Horizontal rules.

Ease of use:

• Integrated toolbar button;
• Command available in the Edit and context menus;
• Customizable keyboard shortcut (Ctrl+Shift+C by default);

All processing is local, without sending data to external servers or requiring an internet connection.
